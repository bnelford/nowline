Open RoomHop

Your conference schedule, live.

RoomHop turns the schedule you already publish into a live timeline on every attendee's phone: what's on now, what's next, and whether they'll make the walk to their next session.

It reads Sched, pretalx, Sessionize, or any whole-event .ics export. Your schedule tool stays the source of truth. RoomHop isn't a CFP, ticketing or schedule-authoring tool.

Three steps

  1. 1Paste your feed linkOpen RoomHop, choose ⋯ More → Load schedule…, and paste your Sched, pretalx or Sessionize export URL.
  2. 2ShareShare… gives you one link that opens your schedule on any phone. Post it in the event chat, the app, or the welcome email.
  3. 3Print signs⋯ More → Room signs… → Print all makes a QR sign for every room. Tape one by each door.

What attendees get

  • Every room as a lane, with a red line at the current time.
  • What's on now and what starts next, at a glance.
  • Starred sessions and their own blocks, checked for overlaps and for walks longer than the gap.
  • Scan a room sign: "You're here", with directions and a leave-by time for the next stop.
  • Automatic updates while the app is open, with notices when a session changes room.
  • Works offline once loaded, and installs to the home screen.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no analytics or tracking.
  • Stars, agenda and location stay in the attendee's browser.
  • Fetching your feed goes through a small relay, which sees the feed URL and the phone's IP address. Run your own relay to keep that in-house.

Details: PRIVACY.md

Cost

  • Free to use.
  • Open source under the Apache License 2.0.
  • Self-hosting is optional: the public copy and relay work as they are.

Deploy your own

Fork the repository and turn on GitHub Pages, or copy the static files to any web host. Add a roomhop.config.json for your event's name and branding, and deploy your own relay with one command if your schedule is on a host the public relay doesn't allow.
